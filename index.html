[ THESIS ]

DINO is the direct interface between tokenized real-world equities, public market eligibility, and direct on-chain DEX pools.

Stock Tokens are real-world equities brought on-chain. The public directory proves which contract was observed and whether its data is current. Eligibility stays separate from directory inclusion. Venue comparison stays separate from execution. Only the user's wallet may sign and broadcast — DINO does not wrap assets, custody funds, or receive private keys.

[ ARCHITECTURE ]

The directory informs. The DEX quotes. The wallet decides.

LAYER 01 — DIRECTORY

Approved contracts — public, sourced, fail-closed
